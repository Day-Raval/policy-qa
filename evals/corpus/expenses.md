# Expense Policy

> SYNTHETIC: fictional company "Quillmark Systems", written for testing policy-qa. Not a real policy.

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1 General rules

### 1.1 Submission deadline
Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the expense.

### 1.2 Receipts
An itemized receipt is required for every expense over $25.

### 1.3 Non-reimbursable items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines and parking fines are not reimbursable.

## 2 Meals

### 2.1 Daily meal allowance
When travelling on company business, the daily meal allowance is $75.

### 2.2 High-cost cities
In New York, San Francisco and London, the daily meal allowance is $95.

## 3 Client entertainment

### 3.1 Limits
Client meals may cost up to $120 per person. A director must approve any client meal over $500 in total.

## 4 Home office

### 4.1 Scope
This section covers equipment for working from home.

### 4.2 Home-office equipment
Home-office equipment up to $300 per year is reimbursable for full-time employees. This includes chairs, desks and monitors.

## 5 Approvals

### 5.1 Approval chain
Claims up to $1,000 are approved by the employee's manager. Claims above $1,000 also need approval from Finance.

## 6 Contractors

### 6.1 Contractor expenses
Contractors may claim travel costs only when pre-approved in writing by their manager. No other expenses are reimbursable for contractors.
