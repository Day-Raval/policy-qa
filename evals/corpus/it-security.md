# IT Security Policy

> SYNTHETIC: fictional company "Quillmark Systems", written for testing policy-qa. Not a real policy.

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1 Accounts

### 1.1 Multi-factor authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required on every company account.

### 1.2 Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across systems.

## 2 Devices

### 2.1 Company data
Company data may be stored only on company-managed devices.

### 2.2 Personal phones
Personal phones may access company email only through the managed mail app.

### 2.3 USB storage
USB storage devices are blocked on company laptops.

### 2.4 Lost or stolen devices
A lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours.

## 3 Networks

### 3.1 Public Wi-Fi
The company VPN must be switched on before using public Wi-Fi.

## 4 Incidents

### 4.1 Phishing
Suspected phishing emails must be reported with the Report Phish button in the mail app.
