# Travel Policy

> SYNTHETIC: fictional company "Quillmark Systems", written for testing policy-qa. Not a real policy.

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1 Booking

### 1.1 Approval
All business travel needs manager approval at least 14 days before departure, except for client emergencies.

### 1.2 Booking channel
Flights and hotels must be booked through the company travel portal.

## 2 Flights

### 2.1 Cabin class
Economy class is the standard for all flights. Directors and above may book business class for flights longer than 6 hours.

## 3 Hotels

### 3.1 Nightly cap
Hotel costs are reimbursed up to $250 per night.

### 3.2 High-cost cities
In New York, San Francisco and London, the hotel cap is $350 per night.

## 4 Ground transport

### 4.1 Taxis and rideshare
Taxis and rideshare are reimbursable on business trips. Rental cars need manager approval.
