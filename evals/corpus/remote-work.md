# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

> SYNTHETIC: fictional company "Quillmark Systems", written for testing policy-qa. Not a real policy.

Effective date: 2026-01-01. This policy replaces the Remote Work Policy effective 2024-01-01 (remote-work-2024.md).

## 1 Hybrid work

### 1.1 Office days
Employees assigned to the headquarters office must work on site at least 2 days per week. Each team chooses its office days.

### 1.2 Core hours
All employees must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone on working days.

## 2 Fully remote roles

### 2.1 Eligibility
A role may be fully remote only with written approval from the employee's director and HR.

### 2.2 Internet stipend
Employees in approved fully remote roles receive an internet stipend of $50 per month, paid through payroll.

## 3 Working from another location

### 3.1 Working abroad
Employees may work from a country other than their country of employment for up to 30 days in a calendar year, with prior approval from HR.

### 3.2 Security while remote
When working outside the office, employees must follow the IT Security Policy, including the VPN rule for public Wi-Fi.
