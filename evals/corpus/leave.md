# Leave Policy

> SYNTHETIC: fictional company "Quillmark Systems", written for testing policy-qa. Not a real policy.

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1 Scope

### 1.1 Who this covers
This policy covers full-time and part-time employees of Quillmark Systems. Contractors are covered only where a section says so.

## 2 Paid leave

### 2.1 Annual paid leave
Full-time employees earn 20 days of paid leave per calendar year, accrued at 1.67 days per month.

### 2.2 Part-time employees
Part-time employees earn paid leave in proportion to their contracted hours. An employee contracted for 20 hours a week earns 10 days per year.

### 2.3 Carryover
Up to 5 unused days of paid leave may be carried into the next calendar year. Carried-over days expire if they are not used by March 31.

### 2.4 Requesting leave
Requests for 3 or more days of leave must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance.

## 3 Sick leave

### 3.1 Sick days
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per calendar year, separate from annual paid leave. Unused sick days do not carry over.

### 3.2 Medical certificate
A doctor's note is required for sick leave of more than 3 consecutive working days.

## 4 Family leave

### 4.1 Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

### 4.2 Bereavement leave
Employees may take up to 5 paid days for the death of an immediate family member and up to 2 paid days for other relatives.

## 5 Contractors

### 5.1 Contractor leave
Contractors are not eligible for paid leave, sick leave or parental leave under this policy. Their time away is governed by their contract.
