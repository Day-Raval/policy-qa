# Code of Conduct

> SYNTHETIC: fictional company "Quillmark Systems", written for testing policy-qa. Not a real policy.

Effective date: 2026-01-01

## 1 Gifts

### 1.1 Accepting gifts
Employees may accept gifts from suppliers or clients worth up to $100 per year from any one source. Gifts above that value must be declined or reported to Compliance.

## 2 Conflicts of interest

### 2.1 Disclosure
Employees must disclose any outside job, board seat or investment in a supplier to Compliance within 30 days.

## 3 Reporting concerns

### 3.1 Speak-up line
Concerns can be reported anonymously through the speak-up line. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.
