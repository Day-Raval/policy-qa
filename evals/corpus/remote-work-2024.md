## 1 Office attendance

### 1.1 Office days
Employees assigned to the headquarters office must work on site at least 3 days per week.

## 2 Remote work

### 2.1 Internet stipend
Employees who work from home receive an internet stipend of $30 per month.

### 2.2 Working abroad
Working from another country is not permitted.
